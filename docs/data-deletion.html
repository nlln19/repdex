Data Deletion

RepDex does not use accounts or cloud storage. Your workout data is stored locally on your device.

Delete all RepDex data

You can delete all locally stored RepDex data by:

  1. Opening your Android device settings.
  2. Going to Apps.
  3. Selecting RepDex.
  4. Choosing Storage.
  5. Selecting Clear storage or Clear data.

You can also delete the data by uninstalling RepDex.

Remote deletion

Because RepDex does not use accounts or cloud storage, the developer cannot access or delete your local data remotely.
