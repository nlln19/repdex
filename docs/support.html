Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Your workout plans and history are stored locally on your device.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can uninstall the app or clear the app storage in your device settings. More details are available on the data deletion page.

Does RepDex use accounts or cloud sync?

No. RepDex currently does not use accounts, login systems or cloud sync.

Does RepDex contain ads?

No. RepDex does not use advertising or tracking.
