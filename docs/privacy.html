Privacy Policy for RepDex

Effective date: 31 July 2026

RepDex is a fitness tracking app for creating workout plans, starting workouts, tracking sets, repetitions, weights, rest times and reviewing workout history.

1. Data stored by the app

RepDex may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Workout plans and training days
  • Exercises and muscle groups
  • Planned sets, repetition ranges and weights
  • Completed workout history
  • Performed sets, repetitions and weights
  • Rest timer settings and sound settings
  • App preferences

2. Local storage only

RepDex stores your workout data locally on your device. RepDex does not upload your workout data to any server. RepDex does not use cloud storage, user accounts or login systems.

3. No data sharing

RepDex does not sell, rent, share or transfer your workout data to third parties.

4. No tracking, advertising or analytics

RepDex does not use advertising services, analytics tools, tracking technologies or tracking across apps or websites.

5. Permissions

RepDex does not require access to sensitive device permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

6. Workout and fitness data

Workout data entered into RepDex, such as exercises, repetitions, weights and workout history, is stored only for your own use inside the app. This data is not sent to RepDex, the developer or any third party.

7. Support contact

If you contact support by email, your email address and message will be used only to respond to your request. Support emails are not used for advertising or tracking.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your locally stored RepDex data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app storage through your device settings. Because RepDex does not use accounts or cloud storage, the developer cannot access, restore or delete your local data remotely.

9. Children’s privacy

RepDex is not specifically directed at children. RepDex does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Health disclaimer

RepDex is a workout logging and planning tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a qualified health or fitness professional before starting a new exercise program if you have health concerns.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if RepDex changes how data is stored, processed or shared. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
