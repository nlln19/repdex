Core features

RepDex focuses on the essentials: training plans, workout execution, progression and history.

🏋️

Workout plans

Create custom splits or start with common templates such as Push Pull Legs, Upper Lower, Full Body, Torso/Limbs and more.

⏱️

Rest timer

Track your set pauses and optionally play a sound when the next set should start.

📈

Progression

Log completed reps during training and update the weight for the next workout when you hit your target range.

📖

History

Review which day you trained, which exercises you completed and what weight and reps you performed.

🔒

Local-first

Your workout data is stored locally on your device. RepDex does not use accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

🧠

Simple guidance

The app keeps the interface minimal so you can focus on training instead of managing complicated spreadsheets.

Privacy-first by design

  • No account required
  • No cloud sync
  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking
  • Workout data stays on your device
